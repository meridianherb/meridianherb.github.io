Why the originals stay on the page

Several of these books teach through rhymed songs — lines a working physician was meant to memorize. Translating the songs flat loses the thing that made them work, so the slides keep the Chinese, give a line-by-line English rendering, and add notes on the commentary tradition behind each passage. The source texts are in the public domain; the translations, notes, and layout are my own work. Everything ships as PDF, ready for reading or printing.
